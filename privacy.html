Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 9, 2026

This policy explains what Zpar Connect, a private portal operated by ZPar International ("ZPar"), stores and how it is used.

Information we store

  • Sign-in email addresses of ZPar staff and of customer contacts ZPar has added.
  • Project details imported from ZPar's own QuickBooks Online company: customer name, order number, project description, line items, job site address and sales rep. Dollar amounts are visible to ZPar administrators only.
  • Checklist answers, notes, sign-off names and timestamps, and photos uploaded for a project.

How it is used

Only to manage and communicate the status of ZPar projects. Customers can see only their own company's projects. We do not sell, rent or share this information for marketing.

QuickBooks data

The portal reads estimates from ZPar's own QuickBooks company. It does not write to QuickBooks and does not access any other company's data. Access tokens are stored encrypted server-side and are never shown in the browser.

Where it is stored

Data is hosted with Supabase (United States). Sign-in emails are sent through Resend. Both act as service providers to ZPar.
